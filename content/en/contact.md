Here are all the ways to reach the Azzurra staff, split by topic: picking the right channel gets you an answer sooner.

## IRC channels

The fastest way is right on the network. There are two support channels, each with its own job.

### #IRCHelp — help with using IRC

`#IRCHelp` is where you ask for help with anything about using IRC. You'll mostly find experienced users volunteering to help newer ones, and now and then a network operator.

Topics for `#IRCHelp` include:

- IRC commands, chat clients, scripts;
- the network services: NickServ, ChanServ, MemoServ, SeenServ;
- how to register your nickname;
- how to add people to your channel's operator list;
- how to send memos to other users.

Before joining you can browse the [IRC Help section](/en/irc-help), with guides and documentation.

### #OperHelp — technical and administrative matters

`#OperHelp` is where some of Azzurra's IRC Operators and administrators hang out. It's the channel for technical or administrative requests about the network, for example:

- trouble connecting to the servers;
- K-Lines and network bans;
- reports of abuse, flooding and spam;
- requests about the network rather than a single channel.

For using the services and IRC commands, `#IRCHelp` remains the right place.

---

## Generic addresses

For matters that don't need an immediate reply, or if nobody's around in the channels, email the staff at <irc@azzurra.chat>.

To reach a specific staff member, you'll find their addresses on the [Staff](/en/contact/staff) page.

---

## Abuse reports / K-Line

If your IP has been banned from the network or you'd like to report an abuse, use the dedicated section:

→ [K-Line removal request](/en/kline)
